### New Rules

LYC003 | Lumyte.Composition | Error | Composition members must be writable
LYC004 | Lumyte.Composition | Error | Content must use a supported collection shape
LYC005 | Lumyte.Composition | Error | A composable type has at most one content member
LYC006 | Lumyte.Composition | Error | Inherited composition members must be accessible
